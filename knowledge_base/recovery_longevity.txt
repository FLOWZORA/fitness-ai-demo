FitCoach Pro — Recovery & Longevity Protocols
==============================================

SLEEP
-----
Target: 7-9 hours per night for adults (National Sleep Foundation).
Sleep is the highest-leverage recovery tool available. Training adaptations
(muscle protein synthesis, hormonal recovery) are heavily dependent on sleep quality.
Practical recommendations for clients:
- Consistent sleep and wake time, even on weekends.
- Dark, cool room (16-19°C / 60-67°F).
- No screens 30-60 minutes before bed.
- Avoid caffeine after 2pm.

RECOVERY BETWEEN SESSIONS
--------------------------
Minimum 48 hours between sessions targeting the same muscle group.
Active recovery (light walking, swimming, yoga) on rest days is encouraged.
Passive rest is appropriate when sleep has been poor or stress is high.
Soreness (DOMS) that significantly impairs movement warrants an extra rest day.

STRESS AND OVERTRAINING
------------------------
Signs of overreaching: persistent fatigue, declining performance, sleep
disturbance, mood changes, elevated resting heart rate.
Response: reduce training volume by 40-50% for 1-2 weeks (deload).
Overtraining syndrome (severe, persistent): full medical evaluation recommended.

ZONE 2 CARDIO FOR LONGEVITY
----------------------------
Zone 2 (50-70% max heart rate, conversational pace) is the most evidenced
modality for longevity, metabolic health, and aerobic base development.
Target: 150+ minutes per week for general longevity (per Peter Attia, Inigo
San Millan protocols, and WHO physical activity guidelines).
Safe for most populations; appropriate for beginners through advanced clients.

MOBILITY AND FLEXIBILITY
-------------------------
Daily mobility work reduces injury risk and supports long-term training.
Minimum recommended: 5-10 minutes of dynamic mobility before sessions,
5-10 minutes of static stretching after sessions.
Specific tools: foam rolling, resistance band stretching, yoga flows.

CREATINE: DUAL ROLE IN PERFORMANCE AND RECOVERY
------------------------------------------------
Creatine monohydrate serves two distinct evidence-based purposes at the same dose:
1. Muscle building and hypertrophy: supports strength gains and lean mass.
2. Recovery from high-intensity sessions: reduces muscle damage markers and
   accelerates replenishment of phosphocreatine stores between sets and sessions.
Recommended dose for both purposes: 3-5g per day. No loading phase required.
This dual role is why creatine appears in both the nutrition protocol (for
strength/hypertrophy) and here in recovery guidance — the benefit is real in
both contexts at the same daily dose.

SUPPLEMENT SUPPORT FOR RECOVERY
---------------------------------
Magnesium glycinate: 200-400mg before bed; supports sleep quality.
Vitamin D + K2: important for bone health and immune function.
Omega-3 (EPA/DHA): anti-inflammatory; 1-3g/day.
Creatine: also supports recovery from high-intensity sessions (3-5g/day).
Collagen peptides: 10-15g with vitamin C pre-workout; supports connective tissue.

LONGEVITY MARKERS WE TRACK
---------------------------
Resting heart rate (lower generally better; trained baseline 40-60bpm).
HRV (heart rate variability): higher generally indicates better recovery state.
VO2 max: the single strongest predictor of all-cause mortality. Target top
25th percentile for age/sex minimum. Improves with Zone 2 and HIIT.
Grip strength: strong predictor of functional longevity. Train with compound
movements and farmer carries.
Muscle mass: declining muscle mass (sarcopenia) accelerates aging. Resistance
training 2-3x/week is the best prevention.
