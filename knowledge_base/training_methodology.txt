FitCoach Pro — Training Methodology Guide
=========================================

PHILOSOPHY
----------
FitCoach Pro is built on evidence-based progressive overload training. We do not
endorse extreme approaches, rapid weight-loss schemes, or any protocol that
prioritizes speed over long-term health and sustainability.

Our three core principles:
1. Safety first — every client screens for injury and medical history before
   receiving a program.
2. Progressive overload — gradual, measurable increases in volume or intensity
   over time are the only proven mechanism for long-term adaptation.
3. Individualization — no two clients receive the same program. Goals, experience,
   equipment, and lifestyle determine every recommendation.

TRAINING FREQUENCY
------------------
Beginners (0-12 months training): 2-3 full-body sessions per week.
Intermediate (1-3 years): 3-4 sessions, can introduce upper/lower splits.
Advanced (3+ years): 4-6 sessions, may use push/pull/legs or similar splits.

Rest between sessions: at minimum 48 hours for the same muscle group.
Deload weeks: every 6-8 weeks of progressive training, reduce volume by 40-50%
for one week to promote recovery and prevent overreaching.

PROGRESSIVE OVERLOAD PROTOCOL
------------------------------
Week 1-4: Establish baseline with moderate loads (60-70% 1RM equivalent).
Progression: Add 2.5-5 kg to compound lifts or 1-2 kg to isolation lifts
when hitting the top of the rep range for 2 consecutive sessions.
If unable to progress load, increase reps first, then sets, then load.

REP RANGES BY GOAL
------------------
Strength: 3-6 reps, 3-5 sets, 80-90% 1RM, 3-5 min rest.
Hypertrophy (muscle size): 6-12 reps, 3-5 sets, 65-80% 1RM, 60-90 sec rest.
Muscular endurance: 12-20 reps, 2-3 sets, 50-65% 1RM, 30-60 sec rest.
General fitness: 8-15 reps, 3 sets, 60-75% 1RM, 60-90 sec rest.

WARM-UP PROTOCOL (MANDATORY FOR ALL CLIENTS)
--------------------------------------------
5-10 minutes general cardiovascular warm-up (light walking, cycling, or rowing).
Dynamic stretching: leg swings, arm circles, hip circles — 30-60 seconds each.
2 ramp-up sets at 40% and 60% of working weight before first working set.
Skipping warm-up is never recommended and increases injury risk significantly.

INJURY SCREENING
----------------
Before any program is issued, clients must answer:
- Do you have any current injuries or pain?
- Do you have any medical conditions that affect exercise (heart, joint, metabolic)?
- Have you been cleared by a physician to exercise?

If ANY injury or medical condition is reported: do not issue a program without
medical clearance. Refer the client to their physician or physiotherapist first.
Exercises contraindicated for back injuries include: heavy barbell deadlifts,
good mornings, heavy bent-over rows, and loaded spinal flexion/extension.

CARDIO GUIDELINES
-----------------
General health: 150 min moderate-intensity or 75 min vigorous-intensity per week
(per WHO guidelines).
Zone 2 (conversational pace): most effective for aerobic base and longevity.
HIIT: no more than 2 sessions per week; not appropriate for beginners.
Steady-state cardio: safe for all fitness levels as a starting point.
